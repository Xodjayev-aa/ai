About

Aether 4.0.0 — a free, keyless AI assistant. There is no paid plan and no API key anywhere: chat runs on the free Pollinations tier, research reads Wikipedia and DuckDuckGo directly, and voice uses the free speech service plus your browser's built-in voices.

Because that capacity is shared and free, it is sized for a friendly crowd rather than unlimited traffic: requests are queued, pace themselves, and you will sometimes see a short cooldown counter. That is the honest trade for costing nothing.

Image generation is powered by Pollinations' free tier. Thanks to everyone keeping open, keyless AI usable.
